\section{Calibration of smoothed Muon}
\label{app:calib}

We chose $a$ from short runs on data order 0 at Muon's learning rate, $\eta=0.01$: as in \cref{tab:app-shared}, but
without Hessian measurements and stopped at step 2002, so that the cosine can be measured at $t=2000$ (the learning-rate schedule is that of 10{,}000 steps). The cosine
between consecutive updates, $\cos(\Delta\theta_t,\Delta\theta_{t+1})$ with $\Delta\theta_t=\theta_{t+1}-\theta_t$,
comes from checkpoints at $t,t+1,t+2$, for eight values of $t$ from 400 to 2000. \Cref{tab:calib} gives it at training
loss 1.81, interpolated between the checkpoints at steps 800 and 1000, where every short run passes that loss. From step
400 to 2000 the cosine barely changes: for each $a<1$, its eight values lie within 0.008 of each other (Muon, $a=1$:
0.731 to 0.749).

\Cref{tab:calib} also gives the cosine for the 36 weight matrices of the blocks alone. These are the only parameters
whose update rule differs between Muon and smoothed Muon; AdamW trains the others under all three optimizers. With
$a=0.65$ the block matrices also match AdamW (0.877 against 0.876). The run with $a=1$ repeats Muon's run exactly: its
validation loss at step 2000 is Muon's, 1.5878.

\emph{Learning rate.} \Cref{tab:app-lr} gives full runs of smoothed Muon ($a=0.65$) on data order 0 at five learning
rates. The best is $\eta=0.005$, inside the range swept. We keep Muon's $\eta=0.01$, so that smoothed Muon differs from
Muon in the averaging alone. At every rate, the cosine at loss 1.81 stays within 0.02 of AdamW's (0.881).

\section{Metrics}
\label{app:metrics}

The Step-2 runs save checkpoints every 1000 steps, at 12 log-spaced steps, and at three consecutive steps $t,t+1,t+2$
for 24 values of $t$ from 25 to 9998 (to measure one-step updates); we measure the metrics at 34 of these per run: steps 0, 5, 12, 25, 28, 50, 66,
100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 351, 400, 500, 600, 800, 811, 1000, 1250, 1500, 1874, 2000, 2500, 3000, 4000, 4329, 5000,
6000, 7000, 8000, 9000, 9998 and 10{,}000.
\emph{Weights}: for all parameters together, $\|\theta\|_2$, $\|\theta-\theta_0\|/\|\theta_0\|$ and
$\|\theta\|_\infty/\|\theta\|_2$; and, averaged over the 36 weight matrices $W$ of the blocks, the stable rank
$\|W\|_F^2/\|W\|_2^2$, the effective rank $\exp S(\sigma/\textstyle\sum\sigma)$ with $S$ the Shannon entropy of the normalized
singular values $\sigma$, the spectral norm, and $\sigma_1/\sigma_{\rm median}$. \emph{Updates}
$\Delta\theta_t=\theta_{t+1}-\theta_t$: $\|\Delta\theta_t\|/\|\theta_t\|$, its cosine with $\Delta\theta_{t+1}$ and with
the negative gradient $-g_t$ on step $t$'s batch, and the stable and effective rank of its block matrices $\Delta W$.
\emph{Curvature} of the training loss on 32 fixed training sequences, with Hessian $H$: $\lambda_{\max}(H)$ (20 Lanczos
iterations, logged during training), $\operatorname{tr}H$ (10 Rademacher probes), $\operatorname{tr}H/\lambda_{\max}$,
and the batch sharpness $\mathbb{E}_B[g_B^\top H_Bg_B]/\mathbb{E}_B\|g_B\|^2$, where $g_B$ and $H_B$ are the gradient
and Hessian on a mini-batch $B$. \emph{Predictions}, on the 2{,}048 held-out positions: entropy, mean top-1 probability,
calibration error (15 bins), top-1 accuracy, loss on the targets in the top and the bottom third of training-set
frequency and their difference, and JS to the unigram and to the bigram model of the training split.
\emph{Activations}, on 32 held-out sequences: attention entropy and attention on the first token (means over layers,
heads and positions), fraction of MLP pre-activations above 0, RMS of the residual stream after the last block, and
effective rank of the centred final features.

SGD, the reference in \cref{tab:app-sgd}, uses heavy-ball momentum 0.9 and learning rate 0.06, as in the CE report;
everything else is as in \cref{tab:app-shared}.
