\paragraph{Gram LC: an update from the Gram matrix.} Gram LC iterates on the Gram matrix $A=XX^\top$ of the normalized momentum $X$, one matrix product per step (each doubling the polynomial degree), sums $S=w_I I+\sum_k w_k A_k$ over the iterates $A_k$ and updates by $SX$. Its weights are fitted so that every singular value in $(0,1]$ lands between Muon's and $s^{-0.3}$; in float32 it costs about 0.84 times Muon's FLOPs. \Cref{fig:lc} shows its mean curves.
